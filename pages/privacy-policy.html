Last Updated: [02/03/2026]

1. Introduction

Your trust matters. , QMath (developed by Kyra Apps) is built to be transparent. We keep nothing personal — no names, no emails, no location. This policy explains exactly how your data is handled.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). The app may collect anonymous usage statistics such as high scores or game duration to enhance user experience.

Automatic Data Collection

QMath does not automatically collect information such as your IP address, device information, or browsing activity. We rely solely on the data you choose to share with us.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Enhance and improve QMath’s features and user experience.
  • Personalize content and provide you with relevant information.
  • Analyze user behavior to develop and enhance our app.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Third-Party Service Providers

We may share limited data with trusted third-party service providers only to improve app functionality and support (e.g., hosting services, analytics). All third parties are required to keep your data secure and confidential. Currently, QMath integrates only Google AdMob for advertising. In the future we may use basic analytics (like crash reporting) to improve the app. Before any new service is added, we will notify users via an app update and refresh this policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as it is necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. If you request data deletion, we will comply promptly, subject to any legal obligations.

6. Data Security

We follow reasonable practices to protect locally stored data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure — while we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. We recommend keeping your device OS updated.

7. Children’s Privacy

QMath is designed for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has shared personal data through the app, contact us and we’ll investigate promptly.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. By continuing to use QMath, you agree to the updated terms.

9. Local storage (on your device)

All game data — including coins, badges, and progress — stays locally on your device. We never transmit or store this data on our servers. It’s yours, only on your phone.
