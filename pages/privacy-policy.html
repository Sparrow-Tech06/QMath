Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the QMath mobile application developed by Kyra Apps. This policy explains how we handle user information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information such as name, email, phone number, or location.

The app currently does not require user registration or login. In the future, if login or data collection is introduced, users will be informed and this policy will be updated.

2. Local Storage

QMath stores certain game data such as coins, badges, and progress locally on the user’s device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

3. Internet Usage

The app requires an internet connection to display advertisements and certain features. Offline mode is not supported.

4. Advertising

We use third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. These services may collect device information, IP address, and usage data to show relevant ads.

We do not control the data collected by advertising providers. Please refer to Google’s privacy policy for more details.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party tools such as advertising and analytics in the future. Users will be notified before such services are introduced.

6. Children’s Privacy

QMath does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is designed for users of all ages.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user data stored locally. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. User Rights

Users can contact us to request any support related to app data or privacy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Users will be informed of major updates.
