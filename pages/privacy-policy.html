Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 21/03/2026

1. Introduction

Your trust matters. QMath (developed by Kyra Apps) is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email, or phone number.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).

Automatic Data Collection

We may collect limited non-personal data through trusted third-party services such as push notification and analytics tools. This may include device information, app usage behavior, crash reports, and anonymous identifiers.

3. How We Use Information

  • Improve app performance and features
  • Understand user behavior
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Deliver notifications (if enabled)

4. Third-Party Services

QMath uses trusted third-party services to improve functionality:

  • Google AdMob – for ads
  • Firebase Analytics – for usage analytics
  • OneSignal – for push notifications

These services may collect non-personal data such as device identifiers, usage data, and advertising IDs. Each provider handles data according to their own privacy policies.

We do not sell your data to any third parties.

5. Push Notifications

QMath uses OneSignal to send notifications. You can allow or deny notifications anytime. You can also disable them later from your device settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to improve app functionality or as required by law.

7. Data Security

We follow reasonable practices to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

QMath does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

9. Local Storage

All game data (coins, progress, etc.) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
